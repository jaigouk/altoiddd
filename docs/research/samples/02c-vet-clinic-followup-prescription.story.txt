Domain Story: "Follow-Up Visit and Prescription Refill"
Type: coarse-grained, to-be, pure
Trigger: Pet Owner needs a follow-up visit or prescription refill

Actors: Pet Owner, Receptionist, Veterinarian, Pharmacy
Work Objects: Appointment, Medical Record, Prescription, Medication, Invoice

1. Pet Owner calls Receptionist to schedule follow-up Appointment
2. Receptionist checks Medical Record for previous visit details
3. Receptionist creates follow-up Appointment linked to original visit
4. Pet Owner arrives for Appointment
5. Veterinarian reviews Medical Record for treatment progress
6. Veterinarian evaluates Pet recovery
7. Veterinarian renews Prescription
8. Pharmacy dispenses Medication based on Prescription
9. Receptionist generates Invoice for follow-up visit
10. Pet Owner pays Invoice

Annotations:
  [2] Follow-ups reference the original visit's Medical Record
  [3] Follow-up Appointment is linked to original Appointment
  [7] Some Prescriptions can be renewed without in-person visit
  [8] Pharmacy is internal (clinic dispensary) or external partner
  [invariant] Prescription can only be renewed by the original treating Veterinarian or their supervisor
  [invariant] Controlled substance Prescriptions have maximum refill limits
  [invariant] Medical Record must show original diagnosis before follow-up Treatment

Variations:
  -> "Prescription refill without visit (phone/online)" (separate story)
  -> "Veterinarian changes treatment plan at follow-up" (separate story)
