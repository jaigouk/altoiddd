Domain Story: "Pet Owner Brings Pet for Examination"
Type: coarse-grained, to-be, pure
Trigger: Pet Owner calls to book appointment

Actors: Pet Owner, Receptionist, Veterinarian, Billing System
Work Objects: Appointment, Vet Schedule, Medical Record, Diagnosis, Treatment, Invoice

1. Pet Owner calls Receptionist to book Appointment
2. Receptionist checks Vet Schedule for available slots
3. Receptionist creates Appointment for Pet
4. Pet Owner arrives with Pet
5. Receptionist checks in Pet Owner using Appointment
6. Veterinarian examines Pet using Medical Record
7. Veterinarian records Diagnosis in Medical Record
8. Veterinarian prescribes Treatment
9. Billing System generates Invoice from Treatment
10. Pet Owner pays Invoice

Annotations:
  [1] Booking can be by phone or online
  [3] Appointment must not overlap with existing appointments for same vet
  [6] Medical Record tracks complete history per animal
  [invariant] Appointment must not overlap for same Veterinarian
  [invariant] Treatment must be recorded before Invoice is generated
  [invariant] Each animal has exactly one Medical Record (lifetime)

Variations:
  -> "Emergency walk-in (no appointment)" (separate story)
  -> "Pet Owner cancels Appointment" (separate story)
  -> "Veterinarian orders lab work" (separate story)
  -> "Follow-up visit and prescription refill" (separate story)
