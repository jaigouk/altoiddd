Domain Story: "Cook Shares Recipe on Community Platform"
Type: coarse-grained, to-be, pure
Trigger: Cook wants to share a recipe with the community

Actors: Cook, Community Member, Moderator
Work Objects: Recipe, Ingredient List, Rating, Comment, Recipe Collection

1. Cook creates Recipe with Ingredient List
2. Cook publishes Recipe to platform
3. Community Member browses Recipe Collection
4. Community Member views Recipe
5. Community Member rates Recipe using Rating
6. Community Member writes Comment on Recipe
7. Moderator reviews flagged Comment
8. Cook updates Recipe based on Comment feedback

Annotations:
  [1] Recipe includes: title, description, ingredients, steps, photos
  [5] Rating is 1-5 stars
  [7] Moderation only for flagged content
  [invariant] Cook cannot rate own Recipe
  [invariant] Recipe must have at least one Ingredient to be published
  [invariant] Only the original Cook can edit their Recipe

Variations:
  -> "Community Member reports inappropriate Recipe" (separate story)
  -> "Cook creates Recipe Collection (cookbook)" (separate story)

---
NOTE: This domain is intentionally ambiguous for boundary detection.
Reasonable boundary interpretations include:
A) Single bounded context (simple CRUD platform)
B) Two contexts: Recipe Management + Community/Social
C) Three contexts: Recipe Management + Community/Social + Moderation
