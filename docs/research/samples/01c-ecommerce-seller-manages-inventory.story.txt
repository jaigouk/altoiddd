Domain Story: "Seller Manages Inventory and Pricing"
Type: coarse-grained, to-be, pure
Trigger: Seller needs to update product availability or pricing

Actors: Seller, Platform, Customer
Work Objects: Product Listing, Inventory Record, Price, Product Catalog, Notification

1. Seller logs into Platform dashboard
2. Seller reviews Inventory Record for current stock levels
3. Seller updates Inventory Record with new stock quantities
4. Platform updates Product Listing in Product Catalog
5. Seller adjusts Price for Product Listing
6. Platform recalculates Commission based on new Price
7. Platform publishes updated Product Catalog
8. Customer sees updated Product Listing when browsing

Annotations:
  [2] Each Seller manages only their own inventory
  [3] Stock update is immediate; catalog refresh may be delayed
  [5] Seller sets their own prices; Platform has no price approval
  [6] Commission percentage is set by Platform, not Seller
  [invariant] Seller can only modify their own Product Listings
  [invariant] Product Listing must have Inventory Record quantity > 0 to appear in Product Catalog
  [invariant] Commission is calculated by Platform, never by Seller

Variations:
  -> "Seller discontinues a Product" (separate story)
  -> "Platform changes commission rates" (separate story)
