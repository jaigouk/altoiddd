Domain Story: "Customer Purchases Product from Marketplace"
Type: coarse-grained, to-be, pure
Trigger: Customer wants to buy a product

Actors: Customer, Seller, Payment Provider, Delivery Service, Platform
Work Objects: Product Catalog, Cart, Order, Payment, Delivery, Commission

1. Customer browses Product Catalog
2. Customer selects Product from Seller listing
3. Customer adds Product to Cart
4. Customer proceeds to Checkout using Cart
5. Customer chooses Payment method (credit card or PayPal)
6. Payment Provider processes Payment for Order
7. Platform calculates Commission on Order
8. Seller receives Order notification
9. Seller prepares Product for shipment
10. Delivery Service picks up Product from Seller
11. Delivery Service delivers Product to Customer

Annotations:
  [5] Payment options: credit card or PayPal
  [7] Commission is a percentage of sale price
  [invariant] Seller must have Product in stock before Order is confirmed
  [invariant] Payment must succeed before Order is confirmed to Seller
  [invariant] Each Seller manages own inventory and pricing independently

Variations:
  -> "Customer returns Product" (separate story)
  -> "Payment fails during Checkout" (separate story)
  -> "Seller updates Product pricing" (separate story)
  -> "Customer disputes Order" (separate story)
