Domain Story: "Payment Fails During Checkout"
Type: coarse-grained, to-be, pure
Trigger: Customer attempts to pay but payment is declined

Actors: Customer, Payment Provider, Platform, Seller
Work Objects: Cart, Order, Payment, Error Notification, Retry Option

1. Customer proceeds to Checkout using Cart
2. Customer submits Payment details
3. Payment Provider declines Payment
4. Platform displays Error Notification to Customer
5. Platform offers Retry Option to Customer
6. Customer selects alternative Payment method
7. Payment Provider processes Payment for Order
8. Platform confirms Order to Customer
9. Seller receives Order notification

Annotations:
  [3] Decline reasons: insufficient funds, expired card, fraud detection
  [5] Customer may retry up to 3 times before session expires
  [invariant] Cart contents must be reserved during payment retry window
  [invariant] Order is NOT created until Payment succeeds
  [invariant] Seller is NEVER notified of a failed payment attempt

Variations:
  -> "Customer abandons Cart after payment failure" (separate story)
  -> "Fraud detection blocks Customer account" (separate story)
