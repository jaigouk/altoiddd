Domain Story: "Emergency Walk-In Without Appointment"
Type: coarse-grained, to-be, pure
Trigger: Pet Owner arrives with a pet emergency without prior appointment

Actors: Pet Owner, Receptionist, Veterinarian, Vet Tech
Work Objects: Pet, Triage Assessment, Medical Record, Emergency Treatment, Invoice

1. Pet Owner arrives at clinic with Pet in distress
2. Receptionist registers Pet Owner as walk-in
3. Vet Tech performs Triage Assessment on Pet
4. Vet Tech assigns urgency level to Triage Assessment
5. Veterinarian reviews Triage Assessment
6. Veterinarian examines Pet using Medical Record
7. Veterinarian administers Emergency Treatment
8. Veterinarian updates Medical Record with Emergency Treatment details
9. Receptionist generates Invoice with emergency surcharge
10. Pet Owner pays Invoice

Annotations:
  [2] Walk-in bypasses normal appointment booking
  [3] Triage determines priority over scheduled appointments
  [4] Urgency levels: critical, urgent, non-urgent
  [7] Emergency Treatment may differ from standard prescribed Treatment
  [9] Emergency visits have a surcharge on top of normal fees
  [invariant] Emergency patients are triaged before examination, even if vet is available
  [invariant] Triage Assessment must be completed before Veterinarian sees Pet
  [invariant] Medical Record must exist or be created before any Treatment

Variations:
  -> "Emergency requires referral to specialist" (separate story)
  -> "Pet Owner cannot pay emergency Invoice" (separate story)
